Microneedling (collagen-induction therapy) uses fine needles to trigger repair pathways in the skin. It is a favorite for uneven texture, enlarged pores, fine lines, and textural acne scars — without the downtime of ablative lasers for many patients.

## Who it helps

If makeup sits poorly in pores, old breakouts left uneven texture, or skin looks dull despite good skincare, a series of microneedling sessions can help. Most people benefit from 3–4 visits spaced about a month apart.
