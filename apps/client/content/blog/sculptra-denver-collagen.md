Sculptra is a poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA) injectable that encourages your body to build collagen over time. If you have searched for Sculptra in Denver or Sculptra in Englewood, you are likely looking for gradual rejuvenation rather than overnight volume.

## Sculptra vs filler vs threads

- **HA dermal filler** adds volume immediately  
- **Sculptra** stimulates collagen; results build over months  
- **PLLA/PDO threads** mechanically lift tissue with dissolvable sutures

## What to expect

Most plans use a series of sessions spaced weeks apart. Improvement is progressive as collagen remodels. A consultation confirms whether biostimulation fits your goals and timeline.
